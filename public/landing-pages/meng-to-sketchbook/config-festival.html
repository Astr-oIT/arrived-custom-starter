About

CONFIG 2026 is a three-day design and technology festival in Lisbon, Portugal — a city of hills, tiles, and light. Across October 16–18, designers, engineers, and makers gather to think carefully about the tools we build, the interfaces we inhabit, and the futures we are quietly assembling. The programme moves between keynotes, workshops, and late-night conversations by the Tagus. This sketchbook is a field guide to the city and to the conference — looked at slowly, in ink and a little colour.
